Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Is it only for bodybuilders?▼

No — we have bodybuilders, powerlifters, strongman athletes, and general fitness. "I was intimidated at first but it's the friendliest gym I've ever trained at."

Do I need to be experienced?▼

Not at all! The owner personally shows new members around. "You'll never feel out of place" — direct quote from a member review.

How does the membership calculator work?▼

Toggle the options you want — off-peak access, personal training, sports therapy, nutrition plans. Your monthly price updates instantly. No hidden fees.

How does the day pass QR code work?▼

Enter your phone number on the trial page. We text you a QR code. Show it at reception. Train for free. No commitment, no credit card needed.

Can I book classes online?▼

Yes — check the live timetable, filter by zone or trainer, and book your spot in one tap. Members get unlimited class booking included.

Can I book a trainer online?▼

Absolutely — visit the Trainer Roster, watch each coach's intro, and book a 1-on-1 consultation directly from their profile card.

What equipment brands do you have?▼

Hammer Strength (affiliated), Arsenal, Cybex, and Precor — plus 100s of free weights and resistance machines.
